# Louis production rig, version 2

The current Character library imports the original separated pack with the registered production replacements in `parts/index.json`. Original source drawings remain available for reference. `head-v2.png` is the underpainted head; the index stores its visible crop, and the renderer adds independently registered eyes, brows and mouth.

## Drawing and registration rules

- Keep the navy polo, beige chinos, white trainers, curtain hair and beard consistent across drawings.
- Register facial features to the face underpaint. The mouth sits below the moustache. Pupil and eyeball dimensions stay fixed across expressions.
- Blink with a moving curved aperture, a brief closed hold and a curved closed seam. Never squash eyeballs or pupils to fake a blink.
- Segment pivots are at 10% and 90% of height. Match rounded joint caps, avoid outlined crossbars and protruding flat ends, and test the silhouette while bent.
- Stationary production poses use 188-pixel thigh and shin bones. Keep the feet planted across breathing and action changes.
- Typing, mug grip, kettle grip and pointing hands have their own registered drawings. Props must meet the actual hand contact point.
- The kettle base and cable stay on the counter while the body tilts. Draw the pouring stream behind the mug so it cannot paint over the cup body.
- Use the native editor to compose scenes and author keys and camera cuts. Review saved-project frames at 1920 × 1080, including blinks, standing, walking and prop contact. Fix visible defects before exporting.

## Editable example

`projects/Louis  A Proper Tea Break.motion` is a 28-second native editor composition: typing, standing, walking, switching the kettle on, waiting, lifting the mug, pouring, sipping and settling. Its 15 layers, action keys, expressions, gaze, effects and four camera shots remain editable. The library catalog includes the reusable office, furniture, tea props, base, steam and stream.
