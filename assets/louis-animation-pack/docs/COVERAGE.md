Front/profile/rear are supplied reference drawings only. Rig capabilities above are implemented controls, not additional completed clips. - Independent hair/ear/beard pieces (baked into head)

- Animated front/profile/rear or full facing-turn sequence

- Audio-derived lip sync (no audio supplied)
- Precise accelerated/decelerated root-motion paths
