# Precise continuation checklist

This is a working reusable core pack with exported studies, not a claim that the entire requested production library is finished.

1. Resolve recorded IK target errors: stairs (Target outside IK reach by 5.34 px; contact/pose cleanup required.); ladder (Target outside IK reach by 1.34 px; contact/pose cleanup required.); lie (Target outside IK reach by 3.49 px; contact/pose cleanup required.); wake (Target outside IK reach by 3.22 px; contact/pose cleanup required.); stumble (Target outside IK reach by 7.86 px; contact/pose cleanup required.); fall (Target outside IK reach by 3.49 px; contact/pose cleanup required.).
2. Paint registered deep bend, kneeling/lying, crawl and foreshortened alternatives from the canonical design. Replace affected segment drawings rather than compressing anatomy.
3. Split hair/ears/beard with hidden underpainting, add independent pivots and secondary motion, then regenerate all exports. Do not move copied overlays on top of unchanged flattened art.
4. Rig mechanical prop parts: button travel, lever handle/pivot, door panel/hinge. Add explicit hand grip targets and matching prop motion.
5. Align ladder/stair contacts to actual rung/tread drawings and add continuous traversal/root paths.
6. Refine floor sitting, lying, sleep/wake, fall/recovery, awkward carrying and dance studies at normal speed and frame by frame.
7. Add a genuine turn sequence if additional animated directions are wanted; reference turnaround images are not animated views.
8. Supply audio to author phoneme cues and verify closures/coarticulation against the actual recording.
9. For large 4K close-ups, repaint/up-res the source artwork with manual consistency checks; enlarging current raster parts does not add detail.
10. Repeat native schema/render parity, all frame/atlas/alpha checks, contacts, prop transitions, loop seams and UI controls after changes. Final animator polish/review remains.
